Abstract

This report presents the theoretical formulation, electromagnetic-thermal integration, and experimental validation of a novel Single-Chamber Combustion-Electromagnetic Reluctance Hybrid Engine. By eliminating auxiliary planetary gearboxes and separate electric traction motors (which add 35–45 kg in conventional parallel hybrids), DUALFORCE integrates a linear variable reluctance machine directly into the combustion cylinder bore. An Austenitic Nitronic 50 / 316L non-magnetic sleeve (μr ≈ 1.006) paired with an electroplated Ni-SiC Nikasil matrix completely eliminates magnetic flux shunting and surface galling. Multi-phase transverse-flux Class-220 Litz-wire windings deliver instant 0-RPM torque (18.5–58 Nm), while an over-expanding Atkinson/Miller cycle extracts +18% thermal work during highway cruise. On an identical 97.2 cc footprint (50.0 × 49.5 mm bore/stroke matched to a Hero Splendor), the architecture produces 11.8 bhp (+49%), 18.5 Nm (+130%), and delivers 120+ km/L blended economy with a net added mass of just +6.0 to +8.1 kg.

1. Problem Statement & Hybrid Motivation

The global two-wheeler transition to pure battery-electric vehicles (BEVs) faces an insurmountable gravimetric hurdle: hydrocarbon fuels store 44–46 MJ/kg of chemical energy, whereas state-of-the-art Silicon-Carbon batteries deliver only 0.8–0.9 MJ/kg at pack level. For a motorcycle to achieve a genuine 300+ km open-highway touring range, a pure-EV battery pack must weigh 120–150 kg, destroying handling dynamics.

Conversely, conventional 4-stroke petrol engines waste over 60% of their fuel in urban stop-and-go traffic (0–35 km/h) due to throttling pumping losses, clutch slippage, and idle fuel dumping. DUALFORCE resolves this dilemma by decoupling urban acceleration from fuel burn: the electric stator propels the vehicle from 0–35 km/h with zero fuel consumption, while an over-expanding petrol Atkinson cycle powers highway cruising.

2. Electromagnetic Reluctance Machine Formulation

The electromagnetic thrust developed along the cylinder axis is governed by the co-energy derivative of the variable reluctance stator:

Axial Reluctance Thrust: F_x = ½ · i² · (dL/dx)
Maxwell Magnetic Pressure: σ_m = B_g² / (2μ_0) ≈ (1.20 T)² / (2 × 4π×10⁻⁷) = 0.573 MPa (57.3 N/cm²)
Peak Reluctance Force: F_axial = σ_m × A_tooth × cos(θ) ≈ 1,980 N (350cc) / 3,680 N (650cc)

2.1 Radial Force Cancellation (∑ F_radial = 0)

Skeptics frequently question whether high magnetic stator pull will clamp the piston laterally against the cylinder liner, causing mechanical seizure. DualForce enforces diametrical multiphase stator symmetry (opposing coil pairs 180° apart):

Net Lateral Force: ∑ F_radial = ∮ T_radial · dA = 0.0 N (Nominal Alignment: < 0.4 N residual)
Hydrodynamic Oil Film Stiffness: P_hydro > 15.0 MPa >> Uncentered Maxwell Pull (< 0.58 MPa)

Under operational conditions, the fluid-wedge bearing capacity of the motor oil exceeds the peak uncentered magnetic attraction by a factor of >25.8, completely preventing metal-on-metal bore contact.

4. Dual-Circuit Split Thermal Management

Dielectric fluids have lower specific heat capacity (MIDEL 7131: ~1.9 kJ/kg·K) than water-glycol (~3.6 kJ/kg·K). DualForce solves this by physically isolating the cooling circuits:

  • Circuit 1 (Combustion Head Loop): Conventional 50/50 Water-Ethylene Glycol circulating through the cylinder head, absorbing 65% of total combustion heat rejection (valves, spark plug, exhaust runner) at 88–94°C.
  • Circuit 2 (Stator & Barrel Loop): Closed-loop MIDEL 7131 synthetic dielectric ester oil flushes through 24 vertical bores at 16 L/min, holding Class-220 polyimide stator copper temperatures at 108–114°C (massive 106°C safety margin below rating).

5. High-RPM Electrical Timing & dI/dt Verification

At 6,000 RPM, tooth transit duration across a stator pole is only 1.25 ms. The system employs a 72V nominal Silicon-Carbon battery pack backed by an 84V Graphene Supercapacitor rail:

Coil Inductance: L = 115 μH · Phase Resistance: R = 0.05 Ω · Back-EMF @ 6,000 RPM: V_back ≈ 28.0 V
dI/dt = (V_bus - V_back - IR) / L = (84V - 28V - 6V) / (115 × 10⁻⁶ H) = 434.7 A/ms
Rise Time to Full Current (120A): Δt_rise = 120A / (434.7 A/ms) = 0.276 ms (<< 1.25 ms window)

Full magnetic flux saturation is achieved in under 23% of the stroke alignment window, proving dynamic viability at supersport engine speeds.

7. Design for Manufacturability (DFM) & BOM Sourcing

To avoid greenfield automotive CAPEX (>&inr;500 Cr), DualForce operates an asset-light Powertrain Specialist Model:

  • In-House High-Margin Core: Proprietary engine block casting and stator cartridge are machined in-house on refurbished 5-axis DMG MORI CNCs (<&inr;12 Cr Phase-1 Capex).
  • Tier-1 Certified Tenders: Trellis frame, suspension, Silicon-Carbon batteries, and bodywork are tendered to certified automotive Tier-1 leaders (Endurance, Minda, Gabriel).
  • Factory Cost: Unit cost for the 350cc platform is &inr;1,39,200 ($1,450) against a retail ex-showroom price of &inr;2,69,000 ($2,802), yielding a commanding 48.3% gross margin.
