Problem Statement — The Market Trilemma

Every motorcycle drivetrain forces a compromise

Riders are forced to give up highway range, daily electric economy, or lightweight handling.

Pure Electric Motorcycles

  • 95 kg battery penalty needed for 300 km highway range
  • 45-minute charging queues every 120–150 km
  • Thermal power throttling during aggressive track or hill rides
  • Expensive ₹8.0L–₹12.0L price tag with zero exhaust note

Pure Combustion (ICE)

  • Euro-7 / BS7 emission walls choke low-RPM torque
  • High urban fuel cost of ₹4.5/km (~22 km/L in city traffic)
  • Zero silent electric mode for green city zones
  • Heavy heat soak in stop-and-go metropolitan traffic

Bolt-On Parallel Hybrids

  • External motors, belts & clutches add +35 to +45 kg
  • Electric assist quits above 40 km/h city speeds
  • Widens crankcase width, ruining motorcycle lean angle
  • Complex dual-transmission maintenance lock-in
⚡ Riders want 140+ km of silent daily EV commute AND 380–410 km of weekend twin-cylinder thrill. DUALFORCE removes the choice.

Electromagnetic Physics & Live Kinematics

Up to 3,680 N pull per piston — 7,360 N twin force

By replacing weak magnetic repulsion with Maxwell Variable-Reluctance Attraction across a 0.50 mm radial air gap, sequenced stator coils pull the laminated piston teeth downward on every power stroke.

1

Maxwell Reluctance Pull: Force = (B² × A) ÷ (2 × μ₀)

350cc Twin: 1,980 N per piston × 2 pistons = 3,960 N total  |  650cc Twin: 3,680 N per piston × 2 pistons = 7,360 N total across 45°–75° after top dead centre (ATDC).

B = magnetic flux density in the air gap (tesla)  ·  A = area of the gap facing the coil  ·  μ₀ = a physical constant (4π × 10⁻⁷ H/m). Because B is squared, doubling the flux gives four times the pull.

2

32.0 Nm (350) to 46.0 Nm (650) @ 0 RPM

Instant electric launch torque eliminates low-RPM twin lag while acting as an 8.5 kW linear regenerative brake on deceleration.
