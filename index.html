D/F DUALFORCE 350
ENGINE CONCEPT SPECIFICATIONS
01 RESEARCH SERIES
01THE CONCEPT

A combustion engine,
reconsidered.

The DUALFORCE 350 is an experimental inline-twin concept pairing conventional combustion with a proposed electromagnetic piston-assistance architecture. The 3D view is a reference-inspired visualization—not production CAD.

01 / COMBUSTION

Two cylinders

348 cc nominal inline-twin layout. Bore, stroke and internal phasing remain subject to design validation.

02 / ELECTROMAGNETIC CONCEPT

Coil interaction

Copper windings and piston-associated magnetic elements are shown as a proposed system. Their force and timing are not validated.

03 / MECHANICAL OUTPUT

Shared crank

A provisional connecting-rod and crank assembly illustrates reciprocating motion and mechanical output.

03PROJECT STATUS

A study in progress.

The mechanical visualization is provisional and replaceable by a future CAD/GLB asset. Electromagnetic force, coil alignment and timing, crank phasing, dyno results, power, torque, efficiency, emissions and vehicle integration have not been established.
