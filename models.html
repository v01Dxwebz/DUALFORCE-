One engine architecture, two displacements

Instead of two separate cylinder castings and two cam chains, both engines use a single monobloc inline-2 block, which cuts CNC machining time by 38%. The 348 cc and 648 cc versions share a 270° crossplane crankshaft that gives the exhaust note of a 90° V-twin in a package that sits neatly across the frame.

Each piston has a 0.4 mm yttria-stabilised zirconia (YSZ) ceramic crown that keeps combustion heat away from the M250-35A laminated silicon-steel skirt. MIDEL 7131 synthetic dielectric ester flows through vertical cooling bores to hold the stator coils at 108–114°C.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from riders, track-day regulars and investors.

Why is the Cafe Roadster 348cc (under 350cc) while the Supersport is 648cc?
In India, motorcycles up to 350 cc attract 18% GST, while those above 350 cc attract 40% (the compensation cess was abolished in September 2025). Insurance premiums are also set by engine-capacity band. At 348 cc (62.0 × 57.6 mm × 2), the DualForce 350 Twin Cafe is a genuine twin-cylinder bike with 46.5 bhp and 58.0 Nm in 50:50 hybrid mode, and it sits in the lower tax band at ₹2.69 lakh. The 648 cc Vector-RR (78.5 bhp / 98 Nm) is aimed at track riders at ₹4.45 lakh.
How does the motorcycle achieve 138–146 km in Pure EV mode without a massive, heavy battery?
We use high-density Silicon-Carbon (Si-C) pouch cells (350 Wh/kg) packaged inside the structural bellypan (2.60 kWh on the 350 Cafe weighing just 11.2 kg; 3.80 kWh on the 650 Vector-RR weighing 15.8 kg). In Pure EV urban mode, fuel injectors and valves deactivate to eliminate pumping losses, and 89%-efficient electromagnetic regenerative braking recovers kinetic energy at every traffic stop.
Will the 2,500°C combustion heat damage the electromagnetic stator coils?
No. We eliminated fragile permanent magnets (which demagnetize above 150°C) from the piston and replaced them with a laminated M250-35A silicon-steel variable-reluctance skirt shielded by a 0.4mm YSZ white ceramic crown. Combined with 16 to 24 vertical cooling bores flushing MIDEL 7131 synthetic dielectric ester directly around the Class-220 copper coils, continuous stator temperature stays locked at 108°C–114°C.
Do I need an external charging station, or can the engine charge the battery while riding?
You can do both! Plug into any standard 15A home socket (0–80% in 55 minutes) for ₹0-fuel daily Pure EV commuting, OR ride in Pure ICE / 50:50 Hybrid mode where downward combustion strokes and regenerative corner braking automatically recharge the Silicon-Carbon battery on the move.
What is the DualForce Factory Racing Club and how do owners join?
Instead of spending crores on traditional billboards, DualForce operates the Factory Racing Club—hosting monthly championship track days where owners race alongside factory engineers. Selecting the Club Works / Billet Edition includes complimentary track membership, pit-lane telemetry support, and priority access to factory upgrades.
