## Design system

- **Design movement:** premium automotive industrial design with restrained European technical editorial; apply it to the existing deck and all standalone pages, not only the new viewer.
- **Core principles:** preserve information; let the engine remain the hero; expose control state clearly; use quiet precision rather than spectacle.
- **Color philosophy:** warm chalk/off-white and paper surfaces carry the long-form pages; graphite and near-black anchor engineering views; soft titanium greys define borders and metal readouts. A muted mineral green/teal (`#526961`) remains the shared-site accent; the reference-style standalone lab uses restrained copper for active controls and actual winding details. Retire fluorescent teal, saturated amber, large gradients and racing-red decoration.
- **Layout paradigm:** original full-screen deck plus its independent nested scroll remains intact; an appended sticky product stage uses the window scroller for the reversible model reveal; long pages retain their editorial flow and original section sequence. Avoid converting every page into the same centered card grid.
- **Signature elements:** fine titanium rules; small uppercase instrument labels with restrained numeric readouts; a single slim progress rule shared by the pinned model sequence.
- **Interaction philosophy:** native labelled controls, visible selected/pressed states and direct one-to-one model response. Group related controls to make a full control set navigable without clutter. Keep legacy deck controls distinct from the new viewer.
- **Animation:** smooth, frame-coalesced scroll mapping with identical forward/reverse progress; consistent easing only for object/camera transitions, not for the underlying scroll position; no independent progress timers, jumps or viewport-hidden rendering. Honor reduced motion by removing automatic movement while leaving explicit controls usable.
- **Typography:** use the existing Inter family for body/interface, Fraunces sparingly for large editorial headings, and JetBrains Mono for technical values; retain accessible sizes and tabular numerals.
- **Brand essence:** “A two-energy motorcycle powertrain concept presented as a transparent, inspectable mechanical study.” Personality: precise, understated, exploratory.
- **Brand voice:** headlines are short and factual; controls use direct verbs, not promotion. Example lines: “One engine. Two energy paths.” “Open the assembly. Follow the motion.”
- **Wordmark & logo:** keep the DUALFORCE name and existing page identity; typeset DUAL/FORCE with a thin twin-stroke mark that suggests parallel coils/crank throws, without changing logos or product names in source content.
- **Signature brand color:** muted mineral green `#526961` across the shared pages; restrained copper is limited to winding details and active states in the explicitly reference-styled lab panel.
